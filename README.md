A phone's last words at one percent battery, written for the make.fun
bounty "Famous Last Words at 1 Percent". It dies as it lived:
mid-sentence, forty-seven tabs open, sending its love to the charger
— the one consistent relationship of its life — with no regrets it had
time to save.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
